## Vì sao học bài này?

An MDP makes the assumptions behind a decision problem inspectable. State summarizes the history needed to predict the next transition and reward, action selects a decision, and a policy determines how decisions depend on state. Build a tiny exact model before using a neural agent.

## Trực giác và định nghĩa

A finite discounted MDP specifies S, A, P(s′|s,a), r(s,a,s′), and $0\le\gamma<1$. For every state-action pair, transition probabilities must be nonnegative and sum to one. Define the terminal convention explicitly: an absorbing terminal state has zero future reward after entering it, with any final reward assigned on the entering transition.

## Suy luận và giả định

For a fixed policy, $V^\pi(s)=\sum_a\pi(a|s)\sum_{s\prime}P(s\prime|s,a)[r(s,a,s\prime)+\gamma V^\pi(s\prime)]$. This follows by separating the first reward from the discounted return and taking conditional expectations. Q(s,a) conditions on the first action; V averages actions under a policy. Optimality replaces that action average by a maximum when seeking the highest return.

## Ví dụ có lời giải

Consider state A and terminal T. Action finish gives reward 3 and enters T. Action wait gives reward 1 and stays at A. With γ=0.5, always waiting yields V=1+0.5V=2; always finishing yields 3. Waiting once then finishing gives 1+0.5×3=2.5. Thus finishing is optimal in A for these parameters. Changing the discount can change that conclusion.

## Triển khai và kiểm chứng

Implement fixed-policy evaluation by repeatedly applying the Bellman update to a complete old value table. Compute the maximum absolute change and stop at a declared tolerance. For this tiny model compare with the exact algebraic result; for a general finite model compare with solving (I−γPπ)V=rπ. Record the terminal handling and whether updates are synchronous.

## Hiểu lầm và lựa chọn

A finite observation does not guarantee the Markov property. Hidden velocity, unobserved contact, and delayed sensors can require state estimation or history. A timeout imposed by the experiment is different from a physical terminal condition; bootstrapping rules should reflect that distinction.

## Tự thực hành và chuyển giao

Draw your own two-nonterminal-state MDP. Give a complete transition table, then evaluate two policies by hand and code. Verify probability sums and terminal values. Compare a greedy action based on immediate reward with one based on future return. Finally propose a partial-observation version and explain exactly which prediction can no longer be made from the observation alone. Keep source, seed if sampling, and raw values in your practice record.
